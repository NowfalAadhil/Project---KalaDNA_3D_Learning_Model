KALA-DNA — Smart Heritage Skill Preservation

We preserve the skill, not just the artifact. SIH 2026 · SIH26214 · Heritage & Culture · Hardware · Team Techno Spidey

Solution

A craftsperson's own tool becomes a sensing device: an IMU, pressure sensor and force sensor with an ESP32 capture hand pressure, tool angle, speed and timing. These form a Technique Fingerprint. A learner uses the same instrumented tool and is compared with the master in real time; vibration, LED and on-screen cues correct the technique (Digital Guru mode).

Impact

  • Social: preserves craftsmanship and moves knowledge from master artisans to younger learners.
  • Economic: helps artisans retain and monetise specialised skills and opens paths for new learners.
  • Environmental: supports local, traditional production and can cut material waste while learning.

Roadmap

Camera pose estimation and AR overlay, marketplace and certification. The demo focuses on the Smart Tool, Technique Fingerprint and Haptic Apprentice mode.

Research basis

  • UNESCO: traditional craftsmanship and transmission of intangible cultural heritage
  • Diaz Mendoza et al. (2023), Sustainability: technologies for heritage preservation
  • Vibrotactile feedback in virtual motor learning (2022 review)
  • Petancevski et al. (2022): augmented feedback and motor skill learning
  • Bateman et al. (2026): XR training and psychomotor skills
